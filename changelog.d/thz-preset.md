- New preset `thz` for Stiebel Eltron LWZ and Tecalor THZ integral units on the serial interface (USB or ser2net) through the THZ integration (`bigbadoooff/thz`). Entities are found by translation key, so entity ids, the entity-id style and the Home Assistant language do not matter. Built from the integration source and extended from a real installation: main ventilator speed, heat stage control as booster, hot water operating mode, return of circuit 1 and the daily COP for heating and hot water.
