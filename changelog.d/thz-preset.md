- New preset `thz` for Stiebel Eltron LWZ and Tecalor THZ integral units on the serial interface (USB or ser2net) through the THZ integration (`bigbadoooff/thz`). Entities are found by translation key, so entity ids, the entity-id style and the Home Assistant language do not matter. Built from the integration source and extended from a real installation: main ventilator speed, hot water operating mode, return of circuit 1, the daily COP for heating and hot water, the utility lock (`evu_release`, on while locked) as SG-Ready state, the low end of the heating curve as curve shift and the relative heating power as compressor speed. The one hot water sensor sits at the bottom of the tank. The booster comes from the heat stage control, where 2 is the heat pump and 3 to 5 are the booster stages, else from the booster flag. A preset can now turn such a stage field into heater stages with `heater_map`.
- The tank tile and the hot water run values (tank at start and end, target reached) use the highest tank sensor there is, so a tank with only a middle or bottom sensor shows its temperature there too.
